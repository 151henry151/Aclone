# Controls and quick answers

Live building quotes and world rules override these defaults. This concise reference also supplies NPC help; detailed walkthroughs are in [Playing](PLAYING.md) and [Economy](ECONOMY.md).

## Controls

- **WASD / arrow keys:** drive, reverse and steer; **Shift:** boost, using more fuel.
- **E / Ctrl:** open the nearby building. Click a building to inspect it; actions need proximity.
- **Space / Parp:** horn. **Tab:** horn unless fighting is enabled, when it fires the selected weapon. Hold/release Tab for javelins; **1–6** select weapons. Tab inside a window moves keyboard focus.
- **F4 / Engine:** start or stop the engine. **L / Lights:** headlights.
- **C / View:** cycle camera; mouse wheel zooms; drag in first-person to look around/up.
- **I / Inventory:** inventory, food/drink, vehicles and walking. **M** or click minimap: **Parish map**, with named buildings/resources; M again closes it. The **Parish Directory** is a separate list.
- **Enter / F2:** chat; Enter sends. **PgUp/PgDn:** scroll chat; arrows still drive when the chat log is focused. **F7:** Field guide. **F9:** game menu. **F10:** world-owner editor.
- **F5 / R:** deploy/return from a carried robocrow. **F3:** reel when a fish bites.
- **Insert / Delete:** climb/descend in flight; carried jetpacks let ground vehicles lift. **H:** hide/show HUD for scenery. **Escape:** close windows and restore the HUD.
- **Mobile:** left arrows steer, right arrows drive/reverse; hold both to turn. Map, Bag, Chat and Actions open panels. Tap cash for needs. Actions includes engine, lights, walking, camera, weapons and every menu. Drag scenery to look; pinch to zoom. Tap Done or × to return. See [mobile play](MOBILE.md).
- **Sound:** mute/unmute; volume in Pilot & preferences. Touch arrows steer/drive; use on-screen buttons where browsers reserve function keys.
- Typing in a text field or having a game window open stops driving input. Focused buttons use Space/Enter normally instead of honking/opening chat.

## What does Parp mean?

A horn sound: **Space/Parp** honks for nearby players and pushes the Hornball ball. It is not a mission, character or currency.

## How do I start earning money?

Find Odd Jobs with M; drive nearby, E/Ctrl, **Work a shift · 45d**. Stay for the 15-second task; no skill needed. Buy/use food and water, then learn a profession (first course 80d/one minute; later 160d/40 minutes, within skill limits), work or buy a business. Jobs pay only after funded production with inputs and output space.

## Why will my tractor not move? How do I leave my house and walk?

Close windows/chat, check connection, fuel and **Engine/F4**, and finish tasks. Indoors, **Go outside** first; then Inventory → **Switch to walking / Return to tractor**. Buying Fuel does not fill a tank: use it from Inventory. Slopes/collisions/weather affect travel. World menu → Return to town centre can recover a stranded player; repeated server errors need the operator.

## Why can I not buy, sell or work at a building?

Get within 18m. Trades need posted prices, seller stock, buyer funds and storage/cargo room. Learn the required profession for jobs. At your own property use **Stockroom** for goods and **Building Admin** for investment/withdrawals/prices; no self-trading or paid self-employment. Owners may farm unpaid. Buying your workplace ends your job there.

## Why is my flour mill not making flour?

Put **5 wheat** in the mill's **Stockroom** to produce **3 flour** there per successful **10-real-minute** default cycle—not in the worker's inventory. No personal “mill flour” task exists; custom recipes/world speed may differ.

The worker learns **miller**, visits, chooses **Take this job**, then **Work two cycles** to renew active employment. The owner supplies inputs/wage capital, not the employee. There is no owner hire button. Default gross wage is **22d per active worker per completed cycle**; 14.59d capital is 7.41d short for one worker. Check active staff, stock, output space, capital and the next-check countdown. Work starts a shift, not an instant batch. Holding a job or standing nearby is not an active shift; reaccepting the same job safely renews it too. Unstaffed production uses reduced efficiency. Owners cannot employ themselves; farms use seasonal plots, and wild nodes do not supply wheat.

## What happens when I log out or the server restarts?

Accounts/property/inventory/skills/tasks/journeys persist. Actions save immediately; movement/simulation about every five seconds. Hunger, thirst and starvation continue offline; stocked homes/unexpired rooms feed residents, but empty supplies/expired bookings can kill. Offline ageing/passive property decay pause; business/study continue under normal conditions. Restart catch-up caps at 30 real days. Return with the same identity, not a new name.

## Why did my business become unclaimed?

Outside arena/comic knockouts, death starts a new life: cash survives, but skills, carried goods and jobs are lost. In Puddlewick, ordinary businesses and homes become unclaimed but keep their stock and investment; names and posted prices remain. The asking price adds 90% of current contents/investment to the base building value, then falls 5% each unclaimed game year. Other worlds configure whether contents survive and how they affect price; hotels and B&Bs are retained but close to new bookings. Check for the parish “begun a new life” notice. Stock your home or room before logging off; offline starvation can trigger this rule. This upgrade cannot restore stores already erased under the previous rule.

## How do I set a password or reset it by email?

**Pilot & preferences:** set at least 12 characters; changing an existing password needs the current one. With operator-configured email, add an address and verify it. **Forgot your password?** sends a single-use, 30-minute reset only to a verified address. Keep a private exported pilot key; password sign-in/reset can invalidate old keys. Never send credentials to an NPC; they cannot manage accounts. [Host mail setup](HOSTING.md#accounts-and-recovery-email).

## How do I keep healthy, store food and use a home?

Eat/drink from Inventory when HUD bars are low; repeated foods help less. Deposit cottage supplies through Stockroom, then Go home; paid rooms use a private pantry and Enter your room. Indoors slows needs by 20% and supplies feed offline. Go outside to leave. Expired/checked-out rooms stop feeding but leftover pantry goods remain collectible. These are exterior shelters, not interior maps; keep travel provisions/fuel.

## How do I build, paint or customize things?

**Build** lists costs/materials/skills/tier limits and cottage styles (including timber); supply/fund completed businesses. Garages offer seven tractor paints at 25d. **F10** edits only worlds you own; creating one does not give authority in public Puddlewick. [Creator guide](WORLD_BUILDING.md).

## What are Hornball, racing, fishing and Ultrakricket?

Activities joins; Leave activity exits. **Hornball:** drive near the ball and honk toward the goal. **Racing:** follow ordered gates. **Fishing:** carry tackle to an enabled dock; Cast a line, wait, Reel in/F3 within eight seconds, with cargo room. Centered Cast/Reel/Stop controls need no menu; click dock/E/Ctrl also works.

**Ultrakricket:** two players, first bowls and second bats via Activities. The grenade-ball arrives after **three seconds**; swing within 0.65s for six points, otherwise lose a life. The sign marks its ground. These are original Aclone timing rules, not exact historical fidelity.

## How do money and trading profits work?

Normally 100 denarii (d) = 1 sheckle (s); internal 4500 = 45d. Local processors bid 12% above producer reference; Harbour offers only 3% gross on finished goods and less on ingredients. Imports cost more; immediate harbour buy/resell loses money. Fuel/taxes/time/stock/capital/custom quotes affect profit. Spaceport conversion is capped; galactic credits/cargo are separate. [Detailed pricing](ECONOMY.md#default-trade-prices-0160).

## How do I produce water?

Build **Shoreline waterworks** on dry ground with its intake in adjacent water. Supply construction materials, stock fuel, fund wages and hire a **pump operator**; owners cannot work there. Default batch: 1 fuel → 12 water in ten minutes; asks 5d, local bids 5.60d. Dry intakes/flooded foundations halt production. Puddlewick has a seeded service; elsewhere build one or import. [Waterworks](PLAYING.md#water-supply).

## Can Mabel help with everything or act on my behalf?

AI residents explain bundled controls/rules/catalogs, but can be wrong; live windows/results override advice. They can pursue ordinary gameplay for themselves, not operate your character or grant privileges. Name one publicly or use AI neighbours for private chat; replies stay on that channel. Unnamed public follow-ups work for two minutes, then name them again. Mabel stays present; other residents follow schedules. Caps, outages or pauses may prevent replies.

Permitted chat is stored and relevant context goes to Jev plus OpenAI (Mabel) or Claude (others); never share credentials. Memory is fallible. Undocumented features should be acknowledged, not invented. [NPC guide](NPCS.md).

## Where are my building’s saved trading prices?

Building Admin shows current quotes and loads the selected item's saved buy/sell price. Empty means no listing; zero means free. Amounts use denarii/decimals. Drafts and scroll positions persist through repeated transactions, but editing is not saving.

## Can an AI follow through on a deal made in chat?

Yes: deliveries and employment with optional training are executable requests. State item/quantity/buyer/price or exact workplace and training permission. A seller must own enough goods; employment does not confer stock rights. Buyers need posted price, capital and room. Trips may split loads; only successful trades count. Ask progress/blockers or cancel your latest unfinished request. Survival/schedules/budgets still apply; other goals are suggestions. [Agreements](NPCS.md#agreements-made-in-chat).

## Why does the wage I typed differ from what an employee says?

Press **Save details**. The live saved wage is separate from your draft: gross per worker/completed cycle (per harvested farm plot); tax reduces take-home pay. NPCs receive current gross/net and total payroll separately; old messages are history.

## Can I give money to someone or refuel a stranded player?

Select their online name or **World/F9 → Players & roadside help** (mobile: Actions). Gifts use cash in hand, at any parish distance, with two-decimal denarii and private receipts. Refuelling uses one carried Fuel (up to 8 tank units, cap 64, surplus consumed), not the helper's tank. Both must be online, outside, stopped, within 15m/same height and free of other activities; target needs a fuel-powered vehicle. Owners can disable either in Editor → Rules. [Player assistance](PLAYING.md#helping-other-players).
